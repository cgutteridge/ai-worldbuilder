# Snags: Southampton cell 48,-44 overnight run (2026-10-26)

Purpose: improve the **process**, not the cell output. Review at the end; do not fix in loops.
Tool fixes already shipped during the run are marked **fixed**; each of those points at the
underlying process gap, which is what to review.

## How to add one (live, from in-world)

Move the player to look at the issue from above, then say `snag: <your description>`. I record:

- **Where**: player position (x, y, z) and the cell
- **Snapshot**: a capture from the player's view (`view_capture` saves a PNG; I copy it to `docs/snags/img/S-NN.png`)
- **Your note**: your words, verbatim
- **Mine**: what I know of the cause, if anything (kept separate from yours)

Entry template:

```
### S-NN  <short title>
- Where: x,y,z (cell tx,tz)
- Snapshot: img/S-NN.png
- Your note: "<verbatim>"
- Mine: <cause / evidence, or "unknown">
- Status: open | fixed (#ticket) | accepted
```
